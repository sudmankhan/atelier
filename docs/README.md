# FORM & GRAIN — Documentation Index

FORM & GRAIN is an interactive woodworking / chairmaking studio. Users study two
historic-inspired chairs (a Kangaroo-inspired Chandigarh lounge chair and a
Japanese-inspired black spindle-back armchair), change their dimensions live in 3D,
see grain/stress/joint/stability consequences, get shop outputs (cut list, angles),
build their own chair from components, match real boards to parts, and talk to an
AI "Master" mentor grounded in a curated knowledge library.

Read in this order:

1. [FEATURES.md](./FEATURES.md) — every screen, button and behaviour, user-facing.
2. [ARCHITECTURE.md](./ARCHITECTURE.md) — stack, folders, files, data flow, state, persistence.
3. [AI_AND_APIS.md](./AI_AND_APIS.md) — how Gemini (Lovable AI Gateway), RAG, vision and ElevenLabs are used.
4. [ENGINEERING_RULES.md](./ENGINEERING_RULES.md) — every formula, constant and threshold in the deterministic engine.
5. [LLM_HANDOFF.md](./LLM_HANDOFF.md) — rules, invariants, how to run/test, known limits, open work.

Honesty statement (must be preserved in any future work): all numbers are
rule-of-thumb teaching estimates. There is no FEA, no certified strength or safety
result, and the 3D chairs are educational reconstructions, not exact reproductions.
